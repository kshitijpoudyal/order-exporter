How scanning works

  1. Open the store's order history and sign in as you normally would. The extension never sees your password.
  2. Click the extension icon. It picks the store you're on automatically.
  3. Scan. It pages through your orders and opens each one in the background to collect items, prices, tax and payment.
  4. Export from the popup or from this page — one store, or all of them in a single workbook.
