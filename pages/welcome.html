Order History Exporter

Export your own order history from Amazon, Walmart, Costco and Sam's Club to Excel or CSV.

Before you start: what this extension reads and where it goes

  • What it reads. When you're signed in on one of the supported stores and run a scan, it reads your purchase history pages: order and receipt numbers, dates, store/warehouse, items (name, item number, quantity, prices, savings, seller), subtotal, fees, tax, total, and the card type with its last four digits as the store shows them. It does not save shipping names or addresses.
  • Where it's kept. Only in this browser's extension storage, on this computer.
  • Who receives it. Nobody. The extension has no server, no analytics and no tracking, and never sends your data anywhere. Files are created only when you click Export, and saved to your Downloads folder.
  • Where it runs. Only on www.amazon.com, www.walmart.com, www.costco.com and www.samsclub.com. It never sees your password; you sign in to the store yourself.
  • What it does on the page. During a scan it clicks "Next page", opens order details or receipts, and switches Costco's date periods for you; on Amazon it loads your order list, order details and invoice pages in the background, just as if you opened them. It goes at a slow pace, then stops.
  • Your control. Delete saved orders for any store from the popup or the Retailers page, withdraw consent below, or remove the extension to erase everything.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Amazon, Walmart, Costco or Sam's Club. Store names are used only to say which sites the extension works with.
