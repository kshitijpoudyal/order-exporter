# Privacy policy — Order History Exporter

_Last updated October 18, 2026_

## Summary

Order History Exporter is a browser extension that exports **your own** order history from Amazon, Walmart, Costco and Sam's Club to Excel or CSV files. All processing happens inside your browser. **The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data.** The extension has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the extension reads

Only after you agree in the extension, and only on www.amazon.com, www.walmart.com, www.costco.com and www.samsclub.com, the extension reads your purchase-history pages while you are signed in: order or receipt numbers, dates, order type, store or warehouse name and address shown on the receipt, items (name, item number or ASIN, quantity or weight, unit and line prices, savings, seller), subtotal, fees, tax, total, item counts, and the payment card type with its last four digits as the store displays them.

It does not store shipping names or addresses, and does not read or store your password, full card numbers, or anything on other websites. During a scan it navigates your order pages for you (next page, order details, receipt pop-ups, Costco date periods), and on Amazon loads your order list, order details and invoice pages in the background with your existing session at a deliberately slow pace.

## Where your data is kept

In your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer, separately for each store. It is not synced to other devices by the extension and never leaves your computer unless you export a file and choose to share it yourself.

## Sharing

None. The extension makes no network requests of its own to any server other than the store website you are already using, and it does not transfer data to the developer or any third party. It is not used for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or data brokering, and no human reads it.

## Your choices

You can delete a store's saved orders from the popup or the Retailers page, withdraw consent on the Welcome page (the extension then stops reading pages), or remove the extension, which deletes all of its stored data.

## Permissions

**storage**: keep your exported order data locally between scans. **Site access to www.amazon.com, www.walmart.com, www.costco.com and www.samsclub.com**: read your order history pages on those sites when you scan. The extension requests no other permissions.

## Limited Use

The use of information received by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the export feature you see.
